# Hi, I'm Morgan.

I'm a Computational Neuroscience PhD student at UC San Diego, developing data-driven tools to understand heart-brain interactions.

My work combines signal processing, Bayesian modeling, and open-source development to study how cardiac and neural dynamics relate — with applications in aging, sex-hormone physiology, and wearable health technology.

---

<h2 style="color:#2857A7; font-size:2rem;">Projects & Research Highlights</h2>

### Hormones, 1/f Dynamics & Heart–Brain Coupling
A multi-session MEG study linking sex hormones to aperiodic neural dynamics, oscillatory spectral parameters, and cardiac-evoked cortical responses (TRF-HEP) across Yeo-8 networks.

[View Project](/projects/#hormones-1f-dynamics){: .btn .btn--primary}

---

### PyHEARTS — ECG Analysis Toolbox
A Python package for beat-to-beat ECG morphology analysis — Gaussian waveform fitting, record-level T-wave detection, and ~136 features per cardiac cycle. Version 1.0 released July 2026.

[View Project](/projects/#pyhearts){: .btn .btn--primary}
