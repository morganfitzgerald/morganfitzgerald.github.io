# Hi, I'm Morgan.

I'm a PhD candidate in cognitive neuroscience at UC San Diego, studying the physiological signals that accompany changes in cognitive state.

My work focuses on disentangling how cardiac activity both influences and contaminates the brain signals we use to measure attention, memory, and cognitive function. I combine Bayesian modeling, machine learning, and open-source tool development to answer these questions at scale.

---

<h2 style="color:#2857A7; font-size:2rem;">Projects & Research Highlights</h2>

### Physiological Signals & Cognitive State
A multi-session MEG study linking hormones and autonomic physiology to large-scale neural dynamics, asking how arousal and endocrine state shape cortical activity relevant to cognition.

[View Project](/projects/#hormones-1f-dynamics){: .btn .btn--primary}

### PyHEARTS: Cardiac Signal Analysis at Scale
An open-source Python toolbox for beat-to-beat ECG morphology analysis, validated across 30,000+ individuals and built to separate cardiac from neural contributions in physiological data.

[View Project](/projects/#pyhearts){: .btn .btn--primary}
